Missing Semester CPU Affinity Exercise

Command:

taskset --cpu-list 0,2 stress -c 3

stress -c 3 creates three CPU-intensive workers.

taskset restricts all of those workers to only these CPUs:

0,2

Therefore:

Number of workers = 3
Allowed logical CPUs = 2

At most two workers can run simultaneously on two different CPUs.

The third worker must share CPU time with one of the other workers.

This demonstrates that the number of runnable processes is different
from the number of CPUs available to those processes.

CPU affinity controls where the Linux scheduler is allowed to execute
a process.
